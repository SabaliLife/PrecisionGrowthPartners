Questions

Logistics & specialty questions

Can it handle dispatch and rental inventory together?

Yes. Jobs are quoted, scheduled, and dispatched, and rental stock or storage units are tracked so you always know what's out, available, or due back.

Does it capture the costs I usually forget?

That's the point. Disposal fees, truck time, and rental wear are priced into the quote and tracked against the job, so the margin you see is the real one.

How much does it cost?

It's built to fit your cash flow — an initial payment, a monthly retainer, and a revenue share with a cap, or a hybrid. See Partnership Structures for the full model.
