Questions

Barber & salon software questions

Isn't a booking app enough?

A booking app fills the calendar but can't tell you what each chair earns, what a service really costs in supplies, or when to reorder color. The system adds the business view on top of booking, so you run the shop by the numbers.

Do I need the heavy modules?

No. All modules are included, but you hide what you don't need — a shop runs lean on services, supplies, and per-chair numbers, and can grow into more later on the same partnership.

Is the barber/salon build ready?

Barber & Beauty is a demo-ready industry build. We show a working version and customize it to your menu, pricing, and supplies as a client build.
