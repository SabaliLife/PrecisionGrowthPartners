Does it handle recurring accounts and one-off jobs?

Yes. Standing service agreements and one-off jobs live in the same system, so you run the weekly route and the bigger job without two separate shoeboxes.

Can it show which accounts are worth it?

Yes. Per-account and per-route profitability shows exactly which work makes money and which is quietly costing you — so you can fix pricing or drop the losers.
