The Story

A team with heart needed an identity to rally behind.

Before

A young club with grit, committed families, and a sponsor who believed in it — but no brand, no online home, and no way for new players, parents, or sponsors to find the program and feel what it stands for.

After

A full team identity — crest, wordmark, and the “Tucson Tough” voice — plus a website with the program, schedule, squad, tryout requests, sponsorship pipeline, and the Badger Code: six values every player learns, lives, and carries off the court.

What We Built

A brand kids are proud to wear.

Brand

  • “Pound-for-pound the toughest in the desert” voice

Website

  • Tryout request form — the team’s lead capture

Content & Culture

Growth
