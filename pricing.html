The Partner Structure

Three pieces. No surprises.

The Partner tier is a true partnership — you start at zero risk, and once the system is built and the business is growing, your investment has three simple parts. You keep what fits and drop what doesn’t.

Part 01

Initial Payment

A one-time payment, settled early in the engagement once your system is being built. Not before you’ve seen exactly what you’re getting and signed off on it.

Part 02

Monthly Retainer

A flat monthly fee covering the clerical work, the negotiations, and ongoing support. So you stay focused on execution while we run the back office.

Part 03

Revenue Share

A small percentage of gross revenue, billed quarterly — and capped every year. You never owe an unlimited amount. Past the cap, your growth is one hundred percent yours.
