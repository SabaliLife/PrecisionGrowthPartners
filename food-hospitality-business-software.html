Food & hospitality questions

Can it really cost a recipe?

Yes. Each recipe is treated as a bill of materials priced from your real ingredient costs, so you see the true margin per item before you set a menu price.

Does it handle catering and big orders?

Yes. Large orders and events are quoted off real cost with the margin shown up front, so you never commit to a job that quietly loses money.
