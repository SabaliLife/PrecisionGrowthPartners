The Story

A great shop deserved a great online home.

Before

Since 2018, Compadres earned a loyal following the honest way — great cuts and real hospitality. But the online presence didn’t match the chair: new customers couldn’t see the vibe, the prices, or the five-star reputation before walking in.

After

A modern, photo-forward website with the full service menu, gallery, hours, and 267+ five-star reviews front and center — plus one-tap online booking through Booksy, so a new customer can reserve a chair in seconds.
