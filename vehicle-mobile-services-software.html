Questions

Vehicle & mobile questions

I run mobile, not a shop — does it still fit?

Yes. The system runs on whatever services and parts we load for your business, whether you work from a bay or a truck. Routes and jobs are tracked the same way.

Can it track parts and my true cost per job?

Yes. Parts and consumables are tracked against each job, so you see your real cost per repair and reorder before you run short.

How much does it cost?

It's built to fit your cash flow — an initial payment, a monthly retainer, and a revenue share with a cap, or a hybrid. See Partnership Structures for the full model.
