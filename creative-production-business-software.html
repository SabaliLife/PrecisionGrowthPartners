Questions

Creative & production questions

My work is all custom — can it still quote accurately?

Yes. Custom orders are priced from real material costs plus production time, so even one-off jobs are quoted on truth instead of a gut number that often underprices the work.

Can it track blanks and supplies?

Yes. Stock, blanks, and consumables are tracked against orders with reorder alerts, so a rush job never catches you empty.

How much does it cost?

It's built to fit your cash flow — an initial payment, a monthly retainer, and a revenue share with a cap, or a hybrid. See Partnership Structures for the full model.
