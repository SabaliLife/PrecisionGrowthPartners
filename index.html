What We Build

One platform. The whole job lifecycle.

Every system follows the same proven architecture — quote, win, plan, produce, close, refine. No module stands alone.

01

Quote & Win

A real-time quote builder priced from your actual supplier costs and labor standards — not guesses. Every quote tracked from pending to won or lost, with reasons captured.

02

Sales, Inventory & Operations Planning

Won jobs land on the production board with a full bill of materials. The system nets requirements against on-hand stock, sequences by schedule date, and turns shortfalls into one-click RFQs to your suppliers — overall or by job, before the truck rolls.

03

Cost & Learn

Closed jobs surface labor and material variance — quoted versus actual. You learn whether your pricing holds and where jobs run over.

04

Refine & Repeat

Each closed job feeds the next one. Variance becomes tighter labor standards, sharper markups, and corrected material assumptions — automatically reflected in the next quote. The system doesn’t just record what happened; it gets better every quarter.
